# Sponsorship

RealflowCloud, Inc. is the founding sponsor and has developed a commercial SaaS implementation. This repository hosts public standards, independently runnable reference implementations, and evidence about their limitations and effectiveness.

The reference project must remain usable without the SaaS, a commercial API key, proprietary source code, or a paid account. Preserve attribution where legally required, but do not imply that sponsorship confers certification, ownership of contributors' work, or endorsement of downstream implementations. Evaluation reports disclose sponsor involvement and reviewer independence. No donation account or sponsor-payment destination is configured without a verified destination.
