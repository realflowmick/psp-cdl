# Code of conduct

Participants should be respectful, precise, and open to technical disagreement. Welcome different experience levels and backgrounds. Critique ideas and evidence without attacking people. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

This policy applies to project issues, reviews, discussions, and other official project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or remove access. Actions should be proportionate, documented privately, and explained to the affected person when safe.

Report conduct concerns privately to the founding project lead using the contact on their GitHub profile. If the concern involves that lead, use GitHub's reporting mechanisms until an independent conduct contact is appointed. Do not post personal information in public issues. Maintainers must protect reporter privacy and disclose conflicts of interest.
